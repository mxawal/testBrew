In the stillness of the night,
Stars above shining bright,
Whispers of the wind so light,
Guide us through till morning light.

Mountains tall and rivers wide,
Nature's beauty, far and wide,
In this world, we take our stride,
With love and hope as our guide.

Dreams we chase, hearts we mend,
On this journey without end,
Through each moment that we spend,
In the end, we find a friend.
